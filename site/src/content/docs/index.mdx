## One line to install

On a fresh Fedora Workstation, in a terminal:

```sh wrap
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/i-jasmin/vela-dots/main/install.sh | bash
```

Then log out and pick **Hyprland** on the login screen. [What it does, step by step](start/install/).

## What you get

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Colours from your wallpaper" icon="seti:image">
		Pick a wallpaper and the shell, Hyprland, kitty, GTK apps and the lock screen
		all take its Material palette -- light, dark, or following the sunset.
		Open windows cross over to the new colours together.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A dashboard on the bar" icon="seti:clock">
		Click the clock: your day and a fold-out month, the music, live system
		rings, and a focus timer that can hold notifications until you are done.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Your calendars" icon="open-book">
		Google, Outlook, iCloud, Nextcloud or any CalDAV server, together. Add and
		change events offline; they sync when the network comes back.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Notifications like a phone" icon="information">
		Open a long one out, swipe it away with a finger or two on the touchpad, and
		keep a history on the bell.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Sessions" icon="laptop">
		Save the windows you work with -- apps, workspaces, sizes, the folder each
		terminal was in -- and bring them back, at login if you like.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Settings for all of it" icon="setting">
		A settings window (super + I) for the bar, the look, motion, notifications,
		calendars and the lock screen. Every change applies at once.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
