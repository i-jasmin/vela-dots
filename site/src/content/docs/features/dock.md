---
title: The dock
description: Pinned apps and folder stacks, along the bottom of the screen.
---

The dock sits along the bottom of each screen and hides until you reach for
it. Apps are on the left of a divider, **stacks** on the right.

- **Apps** -- the ones you pin, and anything else that is running. Click to
  open or go to it.
- **Stacks** -- a folder at a glance, newest first: Downloads and the Trash to
  start with. Click one to open it out.

**Settings → Bar** turns the dock on or off, chooses whether it hides, and
edits the pinned apps and the stacks.
